
Linear Equations Practice

Example 1: Solve 4x - 6 = 10
Step 1: Add 6 to both sides: 4x = 16
Step 2: Divide by 4: x = 4
Check: 4(4) - 6 = 10 ✓

Example 2: Solve 3(2y + 1) - 5 = 13
Step 1: Distribute: 6y + 3 - 5 = 13
Step 2: Simplify: 6y - 2 = 13
Step 3: Add 2: 6y = 15
Step 4: Divide: y = 2.5
Check: 3(2(2.5) + 1) - 5 = 13 ✓

Example 3: Solve z/4 + 2 = 5
Step 1: Subtract 2: z/4 = 3
Step 2: Multiply by 4: z = 12
Check: 12/4 + 2 = 5 ✓

Example 4: Age Problem
Five years ago, John's age was twice Sarah's. Now, John is 25. How old is Sarah?
Let s = Sarah's current age.
Five years ago: John was 20, Sarah s - 5.
Equation: 20 = 2(s - 5)
Solve: 20 = 2s - 10
Add 10: 30 = 2s
Divide: s = 15
Sarah is 15 years old.

Word Problems

A movie ticket costs $12, and popcorn is $5. You spend $38 total. How many popcorns did you buy?
Let p = number of popcorns.
Equation: 12 + 5p = 38
Subtract 12: 5p = 26
Divide: p = 5.2 (buy 5, as you can't buy a fraction)

A rectangle's length is 5 more than width. Perimeter is 34. Find dimensions.
Let w = width. Length = w + 5.
Perimeter: 2w + 2(w + 5) = 34
Simplify: 2w + 2w + 10 = 34
Combine: 4w + 10 = 34
Subtract 10: 4w = 24
Divide: w = 6
Width is 6, length is 11.
